You may need to cancel your golf game, but you wont need to worry about your building. AL13™ is an AAMA 508 tested pressure - equalized rain screen system.
 
Voluntary Test Method and Specification for Pressure Equalized Rain Screen Wall Cladding Systems
 
 
 
AL13™'s unique tab-over design consists of five different aluminum extruded profiles. The panel spacers and extrusions work together to create a pressure equalized rain screen system, allowing any moisture to exit. On top of this installation is simple and allows the configuration to be tailored on site.

The bottom line... Maximum weather protection and the end of waiting for prefabricated panels.
